WINGS OF FURY for MSX2+ - version {version} (preview: mission 1)
=================================================================

Adapted from the Amiga version of Wings of Fury (Broderbund 1987, 1990;
programming Colin Fox, graphics John Boechler, music Kris Hatlelid).
Ported by pfuentes69 (& Claude).

This is a non-commercial fan port, made for private testing. The
original game and its graphics, music and text belong to their owners.


THE FILE
--------
WOFMSX2P.ROM   512 KB ROM image, ASCII8 mapper.
               SHA-256 {sha256}


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- An MSX2+ or MSX turbo R. It does not run on an MSX2: it needs the
  V9958's horizontal scroll.
- 128 KB of VRAM and 64 KB of RAM (all MSX2+ machines have them).
- A way to run a 512 KB ROM with the ASCII8 mapper: a flash cartridge
  (MegaFlashROM SCC+ SD, Carnivore2 and others: choose "ASCII8" if the
  loader asks or guesses wrong) or an emulator (openMSX:
  -romtype ASCII8).
- MSX-MUSIC is optional. When it is there (built into most MSX2+
  machines, or an FM-PAC), the music and effects use it; otherwise they
  play on the PSG.
- 50 Hz and 60 Hz machines both work.


WHAT IS IN THIS PREVIEW
-----------------------
- The intro (title, credits, story), rank selection, the briefing and
  the weapon selection, with the original's music.
- Mission 1: the carrier, the island with its huts, dugouts, soldiers
  and guns, the torpedo planes, bombs, rockets and the torpedo, landing
  and the hold (rearm, refuel), crashes, sinking and wrecks, game over.
- After mission 1 the next missions replay its map: the other maps are
  not in yet.
- No saved games and no high-score table yet.


CONTROLS (keyboard, or a joystick in port 1)
--------------------------------------------
Cursor keys / stick    fly. On deck, Left/Right roll and turn the plane.
                       In the air, Up/Down climb and dive; pushing the
                       other way turns the plane round.
Space / button 1       guns
N or GRAPH / button 2  the weapon chosen (bombs, rockets or torpedo)
Return / a button      menus; on the intro, the next screen
Esc                    pause (on the intro: straight to rank selection)
Ctrl+R                 end the game
Ctrl+F                 invert the joystick's up and down
F1 (in flight)         cheat, not in the original: refill the weapon

Taking off: hold Left to roll off the bow, then Up+Left to climb.
Landing: come in from the right, low over the sea, and hold Up alone
onto the deck; keep holding it so the hook catches a cable. Stop on the
lift and press a fire button to go down into the hold and rearm.


PLEASE REPORT (real hardware)
-----------------------------
- The machine, the cartridge or loader, and whether it boots.
- Whether the title shows first and the music plays (FM or PSG).
- Any flicker or stray lines at the top of the screen when climbing
  high or coming down, or at the dashboard's top edge.
- The sound effects and the engine.
- The speed: the game draws about 7.5 frames a second at 60 Hz (6.25 at
  50 Hz); does it feel the same as in an emulator, or slower?
- Any hang or crash, and what you were doing.
